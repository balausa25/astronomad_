Mission Details

🔭

James Webb Space Telescope (JWST)

Launched in December 2021, Webb is the most powerful space telescope ever built. Orbiting 1.5 million km from Earth, it observes the universe in infrared light, revealing the first galaxies, studying exoplanet atmospheres, and capturing stunning images of star birth and death. Its golden mirror is 6.5 meters wide — over 6 times larger than Hubble's.

🔴

Perseverance Mars Rover

Landing in Jezero Crater in February 2021, Perseverance is searching for signs of ancient microbial life. It has collected rock samples for future return, deployed the Ingenuity helicopter (first powered flight on another planet), and is testing technology for human missions to Mars. It also produces oxygen from the Martian atmosphere using the MOXIE instrument.

🌕

Artemis Program

NASA's Artemis program aims to return humans to the Moon and establish a sustainable presence. Artemis II sent astronauts around the Moon for the first time in over 50 years, capturing 11,000+ high-resolution images. Artemis III will land the first woman and person of color on the lunar surface. The program will also build the Gateway space station in lunar orbit.

🪐

Juno — Jupiter Orbiter

Since 2016, Juno has been studying Jupiter's atmosphere, magnetic field, and interior. It discovered that Jupiter's poles are covered in massive cyclones, measured the depth of the Great Red Spot, and revealed that the planet's core is "fuzzy" — not solid like previously thought. Juno's solar panels are the largest ever flown on a planetary mission.

☀️

Parker Solar Probe

The first spacecraft to "touch" the Sun, Parker has flown through the Sun's corona, sampling particles and magnetic fields. It has revealed new details about solar wind, coronal heating, and space weather — helping protect Earth's technology from solar storms. It travels at 690,000 km/h, making it the fastest human-made object ever.

🌌

Voyager 1 & 2

Launched in 1977, the Voyager probes explored the outer planets and are now in interstellar space. Voyager 1 is the most distant human-made object, over 24 billion kilometers from Earth. Both carry the "Golden Record," a message to any future discoverers.
